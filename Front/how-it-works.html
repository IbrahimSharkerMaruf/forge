How it works

Three tools, one goal: turn ideas into something you can point to

Forge doesn't try to do everything. It covers three steps a student actually goes through — figuring out what a role pays, finding one, and building something real along the way.

1. Predict a salary

Enter an age, education level, job title and years of experience and a CatBoost model trained on real salary data gives a point estimate with a likely range — plus a full breakdown of which inputs pushed the number up or down, and a few "what if" scenarios. No login required.

2. Find real listings

The same job title feeds straight into live job listings pulled from Adzuna, so the prediction isn't just an abstract number — you can immediately see who's actually hiring for it.

3. Build something with other students

Post a project, list the skills you need, and let other students apply. Message applicants directly, accept the ones you want to work with, and mark the project completed when it's done — everyone involved gets credited on it.
