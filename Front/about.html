About Forge

A university dissertation project that turned into something worth using

Forge started as a salary-prediction model for a Computing Systems dissertation, and grew into a small platform: a salary predictor backed by a real CatBoost model, a job search tool, and a place for students to actually finish projects together instead of leaving them half-built.

Forge is built and maintained by one person as part of a university dissertation — the model, the API, and everything around it. If something looks broken or you've got an idea for it, get in touch.

Why Forge exists

Every student has a project folder full of half-finished ideas, and a CV with nothing to show for them. Forge exists to close that gap — give those ideas a place to get finished, with collaborators, and a model behind it that's evaluated honestly rather than just demoed.
