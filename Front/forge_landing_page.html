Three tools, one purpose

Everything between "I have an idea" and "I have a job."

Most platforms solve one piece of the early-career puzzle. Forge was built because the gap between them is where most students get stuck.

01 / Know your worth

Salary Predictor

A CatBoost model trained on real compensation data estimates what a role should pay, then explains exactly why — by age, education, title, and experience.

Try the model →
02 / Find the role

Job Finder

Live listings pulled in from real job boards, filtered by your predicted range — so you're applying to roles that actually match what you're worth.

See open roles →
03 / Build something real

Project Collaboration

Post an idea that needs more hands, or join someone else's. No pay required up front — full credit, a real portfolio piece, and a reference either way.

Browse projects →
How a project actually moves

From idea to portfolio piece in four steps.

01

Post or browse

Describe what you're building and what kind of help you need — or scroll open projects that match your skills.

02

Match & agree terms

Message directly, agree on scope and credit upfront. No payment required — the deal is experience for contribution.

03

Build together

Track progress, share updates, and ship the thing — with both names attached to it from day one.

04

Walk away with proof

A finished project, a public credit, and a collaborator who can vouch for you when it's time to apply.
